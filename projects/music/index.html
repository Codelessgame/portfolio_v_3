Problem & Motivation

Traditional audio analysis often relies on static convolutional filters that struggle to capture long-range harmonic dependencies, chord progressions, and rhythmic motifs across time.

architecture

System Architecture & Engineering

Audio signals are converted into log-mel spectrogram frames. A positional-encoded multi-head self-attention module computes attention weight matrices between different time slices, revealing which bars and frequencies govern the musical structure.

tune

Engineering Challenges & Solutions

boltHigh Dimensionality of Raw Audio

Raw audio sequences consume huge memory. Solved by tuning hop length and STFT windowing to balance temporal resolution with compute footprint.

boltAttention Map Interpretability

Extracted attention matrices directly from intermediate PyTorch layers to render intuitive 2D heatmaps correlating with beat drops and chord shifts.

verified

Key Results & Performance

Successfully highlighted recurring motif correlations and achieved robust genre/pattern classification with transparent attention map exports.
