About Me

Hi, I'm Stanislav, and welcome to my portfolio. Below this section, you can see a visual timeline of my experience and the things I consider most relevant to job applications.

But a timeline alone doesn't paint the whole picture, so let me fill in the gaps. I love engineering and well, almost anything that has to do with math, programming, or mechanical engineering. Lately, I've been diving deep into building AI models. When I need to relax or just have fun, I like to create art – you can check out my best pieces over on my Instagram.

Another part of this website is "My Library," where you can find hobby projects like my "Model Railways Automatic Train Switches," where I taught myself how to make circuit boards. Alongside the projects, I've linked the books I've read and the YouTube channels and podcasts I highly recommend.

(Note: This site is very much in development and some features use AI placeholders — this mainly concerns text written for projects in the library section, altough reading these text still gives the general idea of what I did. Another work in progress is Printables, as I haven't uploaded many of my projects to the platform just yet.)
